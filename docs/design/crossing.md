# The crossing caravan

Restoring the beacon gives the families a light to follow.
  The gate stays shut while spores drift out from South Basin.
  The fifth eligible Hushcap defeat opens the way home.

The first wagon brings Isera and Vey.
  Tovan and Orla follow with the second.
  Velliks haul the wagons by draught poles.
  Their alternating tripods keep three crescent feet on the ice as they pull.
  A forked crown senses the ice and turns in the wind at rest.

The gate leaf rises with its kinematic collider and the caravan stages on supported ground beyond it.
  The wagons start when the player approaches and part into two parking bays inside the gate.
  Each wagon moves on its own route and drives its animal, wheels, and walkers from its travel.
  When both wagons reach their bays, the party settles into place and all four dialogue profiles become available.

The Listening Flats form a separate shelf beyond the gate.
  The main road reaches a loop around ice formations, a cairn made from old wagon bells, and a raised overlook.
  Solid cliff meshes extend around the shelf and join the gate shoulders.
  The terrain and its collider scene load together with the outpost before play begins.

Tovan’s examination reads the live backpack and records the final objective once.
  The encounter ledger counts combat defeats from quest activation, so looting a corpse or loading one adds no credit.
  A checkpoint restores a parked caravan in its bays, or places a pending arrival on the staging road.
