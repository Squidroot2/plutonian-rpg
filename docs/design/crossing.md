# The crossing caravan

Restoring the beacon gives the families a light to follow.
  The gate stays shut while spores drift out from South Basin.
  The fifth eligible Hushcap defeat opens the way home.

The first wagon brings Isera and Vey.
  Tovan and Orla follow with the second.
  Velliks haul the wagons by alloy draught rails.
  The wagon frames and cleated disc wheels use salvaged Relay alloy.
  Fired basalt ceramic shields the cargo,
  and woven basalt fibre stretches over the canopy ribs.
  Their alternating tripods keep three crescent feet on the ice as they pull.
  A forked crown senses the ice and turns in the wind at rest.

The gate leaf rises with its kinematic collider and the caravan stages on supported ground beyond it.
  The wagons start when the player approaches and part into two parking bays inside the gate.
  Each wagon moves on its own route and drives its animal, wheels, and walkers from its travel.
  When both wagons reach their bays, the four villagers leave in staggered order.
  They walk around the parked rigs and branch from the courtyard toward their homes.
  Each villager’s dialogue opens when they reach their home.

The Listening Flats form a separate shelf beyond the gate.
  The main road reaches a loop around ice formations, a cairn made from old wagon bells, and a raised overlook.
  Solid cliff meshes extend around the shelf and join the gate shoulders.
  The terrain and its collider scene load together with the outpost before play begins.

Tovan’s examination reads the live backpack and records the final objective once.
  The encounter ledger counts combat defeats from quest activation, so looting a corpse or loading one adds no credit.
  A checkpoint restores the parked wagons in their bays, or places a pending arrival on the staging road.

## Settling in Nivalis

Caravan speakers use the shared Plutonian attributes and equipment.
When the wagons park, the villagers become damageable and can flee.
A villager who flees before reaching home returns to the walk when it is safe.
Settled residents return to their standing positions through their entrances.
Sella and Orren take up new posts outside their shared barracks.
Nemi tends the beacon from the courtyard.
Her hut sits south of the player’s shelter with its hatch facing back toward the village,
  leaving the starting doorway and the route to South Basin open.

The village’s ceramic shells have open, ramped hatches, supported decks, and solid large furnishings.
Isera and Vey share two cots.
Tovan, Orla, and Nemi each have one, and the barracks has two.
The guards sleep at the rear of the barracks,
  leaving the aisle from the hatch to the weapon rack clear.
Amber overhead lamps light the rack and the bunks in separate pools,
  echoing the amber panes behind the viewport grilles.
Both rack slots hold a weapon upright by its grip,
  so a guard can reach a shortsword or a polearm at the same height.
The player’s own cot is the save point.

The bed checkpoint preserves health, defeated poses, and active flee state.
A peaceful survivor restores at home if the wagons had parked, including a villager still on the walk at save time.
If Tovan dies before examining the samples, use the blue-lit inspection bench inside his workshop with five Mycelium in the bag.
Both routes complete the same objective and preserve the samples.
