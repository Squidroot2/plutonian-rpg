# Story

This is an intentionally incomplete outline of the main story,
grounded in the events currently present in the game.
The setting's background is collected separately in [Lore](Lore.md).

## A light for the crossing

The player wakes in a tent in Nivalis to find its beacon dark
and families waiting to cross the ice.
Nemi, the keeper of the light, directs the player along the blue waystones
to a charged core in a cavern.
Returning it allows Nemi to relight the beacon.
If the player attacks Nemi and loses that help,
the core can instead be installed at the beacon by the player.

## The first watch

The restored beacon also powers the hatch to the Old Relay.
Inside, the player reaches the archive and reads the first survey log.
It reveals that the beacon once guided survey crews to shelter
and still carries that promise for families crossing the ice.
A Hushcap in the archive points toward another danger.

## The way home

Spores from South Basin have reached the gate approaches,
preventing the caravan's velliks from pulling through.
Nemi can explain the danger;
the survey log's warning provides a direction when Nemi is unavailable.
After the player defeats five Hushcaps in the basin caverns,
the gate opens and the families return to Nivalis.
Tovan examines five Mycelium samples and finds that their threads
grew toward the warm gate channels.
If Tovan is dead, his workshop bench provides another way to examine them.

## Beyond the crossing

The story beyond the caravan's return is not yet decided.
The strange sounds on the ice and the broader significance of the Relay
remain possibilities rather than resolved plot points.
The current sequence is implemented in the
[quest catalog](../../data/quests/catalog.quest.ron) and
[dialogue catalog](../../data/dialogue/catalog.dialogue.ron);
see [Quests](quests.md) for their runtime behavior.
