# South Basin and Hushcap Caverns

The outpost has a southbound route opposite the core trail.
  Two paths around an ice-fin field lead behind a ridge about 90 meters from the starting tent.
  The ridge hides the mouth from the settlement at the maximum camera orbit distance.
  The reveal comes after the player rounds the bend.

The cave descends through a winding tunnel into three chambers.
  The central chamber splits around a rock column and rejoins before a tall final gallery.
  Pale fungal cups and pocket lighting mark passages.
  Solid rock breaks sightlines so the player can control exposure to Hushcap spit.
  The first chamber has two Hushcaps and an ice worm.
  The second has three Hushcaps, an ice worm, and a relay roach.
  The gallery has three Hushcaps and a relay roach.

The cave is part of the continuous surface world.
  World position drives the basin and cave location labels.
  The separate visual and collision scenes load with the outpost,
  and play waits for the collision hierarchy.
  Enemy placement is shared by spawning and save restore,
  and stable encounter IDs track health, loot, and XP contributions in bed saves.
