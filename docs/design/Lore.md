# Lore

This is a starting point for the world of *The Plutonian*,
not a complete history.
Pluto is fictional and stylized rather than a hard-science prediction:
its dark, mysterious ice is a place for alien life, shelter, and unsettling hints.

## Nivalis and the long dark

Nivalis is a small settlement on the ice,
and the last warm door for a long way across it.
During the long dark, its crossing beacon guides families home.
Paired blue waystones mark a safe trail north from the outpost;
beyond it, fresh snow can hide cracks in the shelf.
Nemi keeps the crossing light and listens for returning families.

The ice carries sounds strangely.
Nemi speaks of the shelf returning footsteps from ahead
and of knocks on the ice after everyone is accounted for.
Such accounts give the world an unsettling character
without yet explaining what lies behind them.

## The Old Relay

Before Nivalis existed, the first survey crews sheltered in what is now the Old Relay.
They placed lamps on the ice to find their way back through the long dark.
The beacon's first signal meant that shelter and someone waiting could be found there;
its light still guides travelers and powers the Relay.
The Relay's archive preserves a survey log from the first watch.

## Life and craft

Hushcaps are mushroom creatures found in the caverns of South Basin
and, more recently, in the Relay archive.
Their spores can drift toward the settlement;
velliks will not pull through a spore drift.
The velliks that haul crossing wagons sense the ice with their forked crowns.

Nivalis's people work with fired stone and salvaged Relay alloy.
Caravan wagons use alloy frames, ceramic shields, and woven basalt fibre canopies.
These materials connect everyday survival to the older structures without
settling the wider history of either.
