# Player Guide

## Starting and saving

Choose **Start Game** on the title screen and enter a name to begin a new run in Nivalis.
Names contain 1–16 letters, numbers, or spaces.
Choose **Load Game** to choose a save made at the bed in the starting tent.
You can also choose **Load Game** from the pause menu to replace the current run with a save.
Press E near the bed to open the sleep menu.
**Sleep** advances the day and restores full health without saving.
**Sleep and Save** also creates a new save for the current run.
Both choices advance the day; the day advances only when you sleep.
After six days without killing a worm, roach, or Hushcap in an area,
those creatures return there at full health with fresh loot.
This replaces wounded creatures and removes any unlooted corpses in that area.

When you are defeated, your character collapses and the screen fades to black.
Click **Continue** on the **Game Over** screen to return to the tent at full health.
The game restores the save you loaded or the last bed save you made in that run,
including its quests, equipment, inventory, chest contents, pickups, and encounters.
If you have yet to save during a new run, Continue restarts that run from the beginning.

## Controls and menus

See [Controls](controls.md) for default movement, camera, combat, interaction, and menu keys,
and for instructions on rebinding them.

Press Tab during play to open the player menu.
It opens on Quest the first time and remembers the last selected tab for the current run.
Choose **Quest**, **Inventory**, **Equipment**, or **Character**.
Tab, Escape, or **Close** returns control to the player.
The world continues to run while the menu is open.

Press I to open Inventory directly.
The quest log records current directions, objectives, and completed steps.
Select a quest to read its details and completion reward.

## Nivalis and the beacon

Speak to Nemi and choose **I'm here to help** for directions.
Follow the luminous blue waystones toward the cavern and collect the power core from its plinth.
The core is a protected quest item and cannot be dropped.
With the core in your backpack, speak to Nemi and choose **I found the beacon core** to hand it over and relight the beacon.
If you cannot get help from Nemi, the quest log records your current direction.
You can ask Nemi about Nemi or Nivalis by clicking a topic,
or moving through topics with Menu Up and Menu Down and pressing Enter.

The sealed hatch at the Old Relay northwest of the beacon can be inspected to add that quest.
Restoring the beacon also adds it if you missed the hatch.
Once the beacon is lit, use E at the round hatch to enter the Relay.
Follow the amber lamps to the maintenance switch, pass through the raised gate,
and discover the archive survey log.
Use the hatch in the vestibule to return to Nivalis.

After restoring the beacon and reading the survey log,
ask Nemi about the creature in the archive to learn about Hushcaps.
If Nemi is unavailable, the survey log gives the warning.
Head south from the outpost toward the lone glowing mushroom in South Basin,
and follow the bend behind the ridge to Hushcap Caverns.
Defeat five distinct cavern Hushcaps after **Contain the Hushcaps** begins.
The fifth credited defeat raises the southwest gate and lets the crossing caravan pass.
Once the caravan returns home, bring five Mycelium to Tovan in the tall-roofed workshop southwest of the beacon.
If Tovan is dead, use his blue-lit inspection bench.
Tovan examines the samples and returns them.

## Character and experience

The Character tab shows your name, Vitality, Endurance, Agility, Strength,
your level, and progress toward the next level.
New characters start at rank 5; attribute ranks cap at 100.
Spend available attribute points with **+**.
Each level gained awards three points.
You earn experience for completing quests and defeating enemies.
Worms, roaches, and Hushcaps award less experience as you outlevel them,
and none once you are four levels higher.
The bed saves your level progress and attributes.

## Inventory and carrying weight

Inventory starts empty and stacks items by type.
Pickups transfer their whole stack when you press E near them and have a clear line of sight.
Inventory rows show quantity and total weight, including equipped copies.
Select a row to see per-unit weight and item details.

Carried weight includes equipped gear.
At starting Endurance, running, sprinting, jumping, and dodging require a load of 60 kg or less.
You can walk above 60 kg, up to 72 kg.
Above 72 kg you cannot move until you drop or store items.

Select a row and choose **Drop**, or press Q, to open the quantity prompt.
Enter an amount or use the minus and plus buttons, then choose **Confirm drop**.
Escape or **Cancel** closes the prompt.
For gear, only spare copies in your bag can be dropped.
Dropped items form a single stack near you and can be collected again with E.
If there is no safe place to drop them, they remain in your inventory.

## Tent storage

Press E near a chest in the starting tent to open it.
Use **Store 1** or **Store stack** for backpack items,
and **Take 1** or **Take stack** for chest items.
Each chest keeps its own contents, and stored items do not add carried weight.
Close the panel with **Close** or Escape.

The Lamp chest contains two batteries and two methane pouches.
The Supply chest contains two scrap and field armor.
The Gear chest contains a field helmet.
You can store the beacon core, but take it back before handing it to Nemi.
Unequip gear before moving it from your backpack into storage.

## Weapon rack

Press E near the weapon rack just inside the Barracks hatch to open it.
The rack holds two weapons, one per slot,
and racked weapons are visible in the rack.
Use **Store 1** for a backpack weapon and **Take 1** to take a racked weapon back.
Only weapons fit in the rack,
and a third weapon cannot be added while both slots are full.
Racked weapons are included in bed saves.

## Equipment and combat

Equipment slots start empty.
Find field armor and a field helmet in the tent chests,
and reinforced armor and a reinforced helmet in the cavern.
The Equipment tab shows each slot and its defense.
Choose **Equip** beside an empty slot and select compatible gear,
or double-click a gear row in Inventory.
Reinforced Helmet, Trident, and Reinforced Armor require Strength ranks 7, 8, and 10.
If Strength is too low, the game displays a warning.
Equipping one copy returns displaced gear to your bag.
Choose **Unequip** to put equipped gear back in your bag before storing or dropping it.

Each defense point reduces incoming damage by one, to a minimum of one damage per hit.
Equipped gear persists through defeat recovery and is included in bed saves.

The field spear and trident thrust up to 2.5 m and use both hands.
Equipping either returns both currently equipped weapons to your bag.
Shortswords slash up to 1.4 m and can be equipped in either hand.
The outpost courtyard has a field spear, a trident, and two shortswords.
Double-click the shortsword row twice to equip both copies in an empty loadout.

Attack with the main-hand or off-hand attack button while grounded and facing a target.
Dual-wielded hands recover independently; you can start one swing while the other is still attacking.
Movement is locked while either attack is active.
Walls stop attacks.
Press X to holster equipped weapons and press it again to draw them.
Both hands must finish recovering before you can holster.
Rebind this action in **Settings → Keybinds → Gameplay → Holster / Draw Weapons**.

## Enemies and recovery

Ice worms roam beside the trail and near the cavern entrance.
Watch for a worm to rear up, move out of its bite, then attack during its recovery.
A worm bite deals 20 damage before armor; three spear or trident thrusts defeat one.
After five seconds without taking damage, you recover 10% of maximum health per second.

The Old Relay contains an ice worm and a fast biting relay roach.
In its archive, a rooted Hushcap spits from beside a cracked conduit;
the service console provides cover, and the survey log can be read while the Hushcap is alive.
Five Hushcaps in Hushcap Caverns must be defeated for **Contain the Hushcaps**.
Rock formations provide cover from their spit.

Defeated worms and relay roaches leave lootable corpses.
Press E near a corpse and choose **Take** to collect its loot;
unlooted corpses persist.
Close the loot panel with **Close**, Escape, or Tab.
Meat goes into your backpack and can be dropped.
